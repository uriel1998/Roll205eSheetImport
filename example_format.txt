Mouthsucker
Tiny monstrosity, unaligned
Armor Class 13
Hit Points 10 (3d4 + 3)
Speed 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

STR DEX CON INT WIS CHA
6 (-2) 16 (+3) 12 (+1) 2 (-4) 10 (+0) 3 (-4)

Skills Stealth +5
Senses blindsight 10 ft., darkvision 30 ft., passive Perception 10
Languages -
Challenge 1/4 (50 XP)

Leap Ambusher. If the Mouthsucker moves at least 10 ft. straight toward a target, it can leap and attempt to attach.

Low Profile. The Mouthsucker has advantage on Stealth checks in dim light or cluttered terrain.

Actions

Leap Attach. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
Hit: 1 (1d4 - 1) suffocation damage, and the target is grappled and restrained (escape DC 13). While attached, the target is blinded and silenced, cannot cast spells with verbal components, and takes 1 (1d4 - 1) suffocation damage at the start of each of its turns. The Mouthsucker detaches if it takes 5 or more damage from a single hit or if the host succeeds on a DC 13 Strength check as an action. When it detaches, the target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be stunned for 1d4 rounds.

Implant. If the Mouthsucker remains attached at the start of its second turn, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or become implanted. After 1d4 hours, the target drops to 0 hit points. The implant can be removed by Lesser Restoration with a successful DC 13 Constitution check, a DC 25 Medicine check, or by receiving 10 or more hit points of magical healing in a single instance.
